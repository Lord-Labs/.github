# Code of conduct

LORD Labs welcomes people regardless of background, identity, experience, language, or ability. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific constructive feedback, and accept correction. Critique the work without attacking the person. Make room for contributors learning a language or a tool.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone reporting a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

The current organization maintainer is [@Lord-shaban](https://github.com/Lord-shaban). Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's public profile to report a conduct concern. If no suitable private channel is available, request a private contact method without publishing the incident or identifying details in a public issue. A project's own code of conduct may provide a more specific reporting channel and takes precedence.

The maintainer will consider the context, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may edit or remove harmful content, warn a participant, temporarily restrict participation, or block repeated or serious abuse. Good-faith reports are handled without retaliation. Ask the maintainer privately to reconsider an enforcement decision with relevant context.

## بالعربي

احترم المشاركين وناقش الفكرة من غير هجوم شخصي. التحرش والتمييز والتهديد ونشر البيانات الخاصة غير مقبولين. بلّغ المسؤول بشكل خاص، ومن غير نشر تفاصيل الواقعة أو بيانات أصحابها في Issue عامة.
